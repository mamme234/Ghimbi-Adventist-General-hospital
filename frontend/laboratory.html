Preparation

Test Preparation Guide

How to prepare for your laboratory tests

01

Before Your Test

  • Follow fasting instructions (if required)
  • Inform about medications you're taking
  • Bring your doctor's request form
  • Stay hydrated (unless fasting)
02

During Your Test

  • Relax and stay calm
  • Follow the technician's instructions
  • Ask questions if unsure
  • Inform of any discomfort
03

After Your Test

  • Check when results will be ready
  • Follow any post-test instructions
  • Contact your doctor for results
  • Schedule follow-up if needed
