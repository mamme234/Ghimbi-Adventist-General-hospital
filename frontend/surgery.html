Preparation

Preparing for Surgery

What to expect before, during, and after your surgery

01

Before Surgery

  • Complete pre-operative assessment
  • Follow fasting instructions
  • Stop certain medications as advised
  • Arrange transportation home
02

Day of Surgery

  • Arrive 2 hours before scheduled time
  • Bring identification and insurance cards
  • Wear comfortable, loose clothing
  • Leave valuables at home
03

Recovery

  • Post-operative monitoring
  • Pain management
  • Follow discharge instructions
  • Schedule follow-up appointments
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

❓

How long does surgery take?

+

Surgery duration varies depending on the procedure. Your surgeon will provide an estimated time during your consultation. Most procedures take 1-4 hours.

❓

When can I go home after surgery?

+

This depends on the type of surgery. Some procedures are outpatient (same-day discharge), while others may require 1-3 days of hospital stay.

❓

What are the risks of surgery?

+

All surgeries carry some risk. Our team will discuss specific risks with you before surgery and take all necessary precautions to ensure your safety.

❓

How do I prepare for surgery?

+

Our pre-operative team will provide detailed instructions including fasting guidelines, medication adjustments, and what to bring on surgery day.
