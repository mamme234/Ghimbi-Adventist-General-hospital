About Us

Gimbie Adventist General Hospital

Excellence in Healthcare Since 1960

Our Mission

To provide quality, compassionate healthcare to all, regardless of their background or ability to pay, in the name of Christ.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in healthcare, recognized for our commitment to patient care, medical innovation, and community service.

Our Values

  • Excellence - Striving for the highest standards in patient care
  • Compassion - Treating every patient with kindness and respect
  • Integrity - Upholding ethical standards in all we do
  • Innovation - Embracing technology and medical advancements
  • Community - Serving and uplifting our community

Our History

Founded in 1960, Gimbie Adventist Hospital began as a small clinic serving the local community. Over six decades, we have grown into a comprehensive medical facility offering a wide range of healthcare services.

Today, we continue our commitment to providing accessible, quality healthcare to all who need it.
