About Us

Your Health, Our Mission

Our History

Founded in 1960, Gimbie Adventist General Hospital has been serving the community with excellence in healthcare for over six decades. What began as a small clinic has grown into a comprehensive medical facility.

Our Mission

To provide quality, compassionate healthcare to all, regardless of their background or ability to pay, in the name of Christ.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in healthcare, recognized for our commitment to patient care, medical innovation, and community service.

Core Values

  • ❤️ Compassion - Treating every patient with kindness and respect
  • ⭐ Excellence - Striving for the highest standards in patient care
  • 🤝 Integrity - Upholding ethical standards in all we do
  • 💡 Innovation - Embracing technology and medical advancements
  • 🌍 Community - Serving and uplifting our community
