Journey

Your Pregnancy Journey

What to expect at each stage of your pregnancy

01

First Trimester

  • Initial prenatal check-up
  • Blood tests and ultrasound
  • Nutritional counseling
  • Pregnancy confirmation
02

Second Trimester

  • Regular check-ups every 4 weeks
  • Detailed anatomy scan
  • Glucose screening
  • Antenatal classes begin
03

Third Trimester

  • Check-ups every 2-4 weeks
  • Birth plan preparation
  • Fetal monitoring
  • Hospital bag preparation
04

Delivery & Postnatal

  • Safe delivery in our facility
  • Immediate newborn care
  • Breastfeeding support
  • Postnatal follow-ups
